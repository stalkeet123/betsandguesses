Choose the mood

01Classic

Trivia where knowing the answer is only half the game.

Every question has a numeric answer. First everyone guesses. Then the guesses shape the board and the whole table bets on where the real answer will land.

  1. GuessSubmit your best number before seeing the spread.
  2. BetPlace chips across the live board and chase the best payout.
  3. RevealWatch the answer land, collect winnings, and climb the bank.
02Party Poll

Prompts about the group. Bets about each other.

The question is social instead of numeric. Put your chips on the people you think the room will choose, then reveal the table's verdict together.

  1. Read the roomA social prompt puts everyone at the center of the round.
  2. Back your picksUse your round chips to show where your confidence is.
  3. Reveal the voteWinning picks add to your score; misses cost you.
