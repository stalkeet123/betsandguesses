Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Plain-language summary

You can play without creating a traditional email-and-password profile. The game still needs session, room, gameplay, technical, and — for purchasers — entitlement information to operate reliably.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information processed when you use Bets & Guesses (the “Service”), why that information is used, and how to contact us about privacy questions.

1. Information We Process

Gameplay and session data

To run multiplayer rooms, the Service may process a display name, room and player identifiers, game mode, guesses, bets, chip placements, scores, round state, timestamps, and similar gameplay events. A traditional email-and-password account is not required for normal gameplay, although the Service may use anonymous or technical identifiers to keep sessions and rooms working.

Technical and diagnostic data

Our hosting, database, security, and platform providers may process information such as IP address, device or browser characteristics, timestamps, connection metadata, and diagnostic information needed to deliver and protect the Service.

Support information

If you contact support, we process the information you choose to send us, such as your email address, device model, app version, room code, screenshots, and a description of the issue.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and synchronize multiplayer rooms, enforce game rules, calculate scores, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, provide support, manage premium access, and improve reliability and usability.

3. Service Providers

Bets & Guesses relies on third-party services to operate. These currently include Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database storage, and real-time synchronization; Apple for App Store distribution and in-app purchases; and RevenueCat for purchase-entitlement and subscription-status handling.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their systems may operate in countries other than the country where you are located.

4. Purchases and Premium Access

Payments made through the iPhone app are handled by Apple. Bets & Guesses does not need your full payment-card details. The Service may receive purchase, product, subscription, entitlement, and provider-identifier information needed to determine whether premium features should be available.

5. Local Storage and Sessions

The app or browser may store limited session or preference information locally so that the Service can keep you signed in to an anonymous session, reconnect to a room, or preserve expected app behavior. Clearing browser or app storage can remove that local information.

6. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers when needed to host, synchronize, secure, support, or distribute the Service. We may also disclose information when required to comply with applicable law, protect users, investigate abuse, or defend the Service and our rights.

7. Data Retention

Gameplay, technical, purchase-entitlement, and support information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service, or to meet applicable legal obligations. Retention can differ depending on the type of data and the system that stores it.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the situation.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect the Service and its data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid sending passwords, full payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information through support messages or player names.

10. Your Choices and Requests

You can limit the information you provide in display names and support messages. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your use of the Service, contact us at the address below. We may need enough information to locate the relevant records and verify that the request is legitimate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we publish a revised version.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email support@betsandguesses.com.
